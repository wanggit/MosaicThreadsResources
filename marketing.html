Mosaic Threads

A quiet puzzle game about connecting colour and uncovering a picture

The idea

Every board is a grid of tiles with pairs of matching coloured dots. Drag from one dot to the other and a thread follows your finger. Lines cannot cross, and cannot pass through a dot that belongs to someone else. Fill the whole board and the picture underneath is revealed.

That is the entire rule set. No countdown you did not ask for, no lives, no ads. The game is about looking carefully and finding the one way through.

How to read the board

  • A dot marks the two ends of a colour. Every dot must end up connected to its partner.
  • A thread is drawn by dragging. It fills cells as you move, and it may cross over your own line — doing so erases the part you no longer need.
  • A finished board has every cell covered and every pair joined at both ends. That is three stars.
  • Colour is never the only cue. Endpoints carry a marker shape as well as a hue, so same-hue lines remain tellable apart.

Who it is for

People who want one clear, calm puzzle at a time. A board takes a minute or two, which makes it a good fit for a commute, a coffee break, or the five minutes before bed. There is nothing to grind: no energy waiting for, no offers to dismiss, no pressure to spend.

It is also a good fit if you enjoy the specific pleasure of looking at a nearly-finished board and working out which loop has to go where.

Accessibility

  • Sound is optional throughout and can be disabled.
  • Endpoints are distinguished by shape as well as colour.
  • Boards scale to your screen, from small phones to iPad.
  • No reaction-time requirements, no timed reflexes. Only the optional level clock, which can be extended with items or coins.

Availability

Mosaic Threads requires iOS 15 or later and is available on iPhone and iPad through the App Store. It contains in-app purchases of additional coins and items, and is offered with no subscription.
