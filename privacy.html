Privacy Policy

Mosaic Threads · Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version. Mosaic Threads has no accounts, no servers and no advertising. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what you explicitly choose to buy from the App Store, and anonymous crash and usage statistics if and when they are enabled.

1. What we collect

DataWhere it goesWhy
Purchase receiptsApple, and usOnly to deliver the coins and items you bought and to restore them if you reinstall
Anonymous usage statisticsOur analytics providerTo find out where players get stuck. Contains no personal information and no advertising identifier. Optional, and off unless enabled.
Crash reportsOur analytics providerTo fix crashes. Optional, and off unless enabled.

2. What stays on your device

Everything else. This list is stored in your device's own preferences and is never transmitted by the app:

  • Which levels you have cleared, and the stars and FLAWLESS marks you earned
  • Your coins, items and check-in streak
  • Your Daily Challenge results and streak
  • Your local leaderboard entries
  • Game sound and music settings

Because this data is local, uninstalling the app deletes it. Reinstalling starts you from the beginning, except for purchases, which Apple restores.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising. There is no ad SDK in the app and no advertising identifier is collected.
  • No accounts. We cannot identify you, and there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • No third-party analytics in this build. Analytics is disabled unless a configuration is supplied; this release ships with it disabled.

4. Advertising tracking

Mosaic Threads does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, so iOS will never show the "Allow Tracking" prompt. This is because the app contains no advertising SDK.

5. Children

Mosaic Threads is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing to delete on request.

6. Your choices

You can delete everything at any time by deleting the app from your device. Purchases are managed through your App Store account; to stop billing, cancel or delete your Apple ID. If analytics is ever enabled, you can opt out of it via the iOS setting Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Apps, or by turning off "Share Analytics" for this app.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what is collected we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Any change that affects your rights will be announced in the app before it takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the contact address listed on the support page.
