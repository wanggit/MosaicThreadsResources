Technical Support

Mosaic Threads · iPhone, iPad and App Store issues

Quick fixes

Most reports resolve with one of these.

SymptomWhat to do
Progress is gone after reinstallingExpected. Progress is stored only on your device. Reinstalling resets it. Purchased coins and items are restored by Apple.
A level will not finish even though the board looks fullEvery pair must be connected to both of its dots. A line may run past a dot of the same colour but must begin at one dot and end at the other.
My line stopped where it should have continuedLines cannot cross another line, and cannot pass through a dot belonging to a different colour. Those cells act as walls.
Level is not unlockingLevels unlock strictly in order: clear level N to open level N+1.
I want to undo a lineLong-press the line to clear it, or use restart in the top-right.
The clock ran outUse Extra Time or a Freeze, or buy more with coins. Extra time costs more the longer you are in a level.
A hint is not workingAutomatic hints are off in the Daily Challenge so that everyone plays the same board. Use a manual hint instead, or skip.
Not enough coinsCoins come from clearing levels, daily check-ins, and purchases. A restart does not remove coins.
No soundCheck the silent switch and that your device volume is up. In-game sound is under settings.
Picture is not showingThe gallery only reveals a picture once you have earned three stars. A partial or connected-but-not-perfect board does not unlock it.

Daily Challenge

One board per day, identical for every player worldwide. It appears once the level opens, and counts towards a streak. Missing a day resets the streak but does not remove anything else. Because every player gets the same board, automatic hints and free demonstrations are disabled in it.

Stars and marks

  • 1 star — board at least 90% filled.
  • 2 stars — every pair connected.
  • 3 stars — board completely filled.
  • FLAWLESS — cleared with no restart and no line cleared.

FLAWLESS is recorded separately from stars and does not replace them. Clearing a level without a restart keeps three stars as well.

Device and requirements

  • Requires iOS 15 or later.
  • Works on iPhone and iPad. The board scales to fit.
  • Needs an internet connection for the App Store only. The game itself plays entirely offline.
  • No account to create, nothing to sign in to.

Reporting a bug

When you report something, the following makes it fixable rather than guessable:

  • The level number shown at the top of the board, for example "Level 184"
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visual

Send these to the contact address below.

Purchases

Purchases, receipts and refunds are handled entirely by Apple. The fastest route for a billing problem is the App Store on your device:

  1. Open the App Store and tap your profile picture
  2. Open History to see past purchases
  3. Tap a purchase and choose Report a Problem

This is the only way a refund can be issued for an in-app purchase, and it is the fastest way to resolve a charge you do not recognise.

Accessibility

Colour is never the only signal. Endpoints are distinguished by marker shape as well as hue, so lines that share a hue can still be told apart. All sound is optional and can be turned off entirely.
